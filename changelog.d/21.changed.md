An install that already carries `INCLUDE_SOURCES=telegram` plus `TELEGRAM_SOURCES` but no `SCRAPECREATORS_API_KEY` was inert before and now fetches the configured public channels from `t.me/s` on every run; set `EXCLUDE_SOURCES=telegram` or clear `TELEGRAM_SOURCES` to keep it off. Channel handles are now validated as Telegram usernames (5 to 32 letters, digits, underscores) before they reach a URL.
