Local corpus files may carry an optional `---` frontmatter block: `published_at` overrides the file mtime for the recency window, an `http(s)` `url` replaces the opaque `corpus://` key so fusion can merge bridged items with the same article seen on the web, and `title`, `author`, `container`, `engagement` and `tags` map onto the item; stdlib parsing, files without frontmatter are unchanged.
