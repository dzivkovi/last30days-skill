Local corpus files may carry an optional `---` frontmatter block: `published_at` overrides the file mtime for the recency window, `title`, `author`, `container`, `engagement` and `tags` map onto the item, and an `http(s)` `url` is shown as a link in the private "From your files" block and collapses syndicated copies (the item keeps its opaque `corpus://` key so private text never fuses with public evidence). Stdlib parsing; files without frontmatter are unchanged.
